_The Hawks’ Nest_ has committed to inclusion in its editorial content,
community engagement, and design. Early on, I opted to highlight the
first reference of the names of each person quoted or identified in
the newspaper. This small gesture recognizes the importance of
individuals engaging in our community and focuses on inclusion. As a
school that draws from five towns, we have a wide range of experiences
and backgrounds at our school. Through our editorial content, we have
highlighted the importance of recognizing and celebrating those with
disabilities, highlighted areas in which race has been considered,
discussed political changes, showcased different religious traditions,
covered aspects of the food insecurity in our community, and
highlighted ways in which our school is fostering diversity of
thought. Although many of these articles are written by fellow staff
writers, I have helped drive the selection of these topics for each
issue and edited all of them.

Our school has several programs to integrate those with disabilities
into the wider community. As such, _The Hawks’ Nest_ has reported on
several programs and events that support this integration.
