Our primary emphasis has been on gaining visibility within the walls
of our high school with printed issues of _The Hawks’ Nest_. In the
early months of its inaugural year, our only digital presence was
through the submissions I made to our principal’s weekly report. I was
then able to connect through school resources to post digital copies
on the website.

A few months later, our newly recruited business manager launched a
[website](https://thehawksnest.netlify.app/) which introduces the
newspaper, links digital copies, and provides a mechanism for
donations. Due to budget constraints, we are in the process of
migrating the website back to the school-supported site. Recently, our
business manager has signed up for an Instagram account and has been
working to actively recruit a social media manager. The business
manager, managing editors, and I continue to work through which
strategies are the most sustainable.
