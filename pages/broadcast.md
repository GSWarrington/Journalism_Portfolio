Independent of my work as co-founder and Editor-in-chief of _The Hawks’
Nest_, I have been volunteering at the University of Vermont’s (UVM’s)
alternative radio station 90.1 WRUV FM since sophomore year. In my
first year, I trained to become a disc jockey, learning the policies
and procedures to stay within the Federal Communications Commission
(FCC) guidelines for alternative radio.

I also catalogued UVM’s extensive vinyl collection, the largest public
collection of records in the state. In my junior and senior years, I
have hosted my own show: “Uncharted Waters.” As part of my commitment
to the station, I have also written several public service
announcements and composed music reviews of new and local music.
Until recently, I was the youngest DJ in rotation at UVM.

During the final weeks of the school year, we participate in our high
school’s Reflective Interest-based Student Experiences (or RISE
program). During the end of my junior year, I supported a teacher’s
effort to start a local radio station at our high school. To comply
with piracy laws, I solicited for and obtained hundreds of CDs from
our local community to jumpstart a music library at our school.
