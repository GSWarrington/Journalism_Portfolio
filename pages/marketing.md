Community engagement has been at the forefront of nearly every article
and all of our reporting. In fact, the selection of a print format was
specifically intended to have a physical representation of our work
across our local community. In addition to print issues, we often post
flyers around school for various events including recruitment,
surveys, and fundraisers. By introducing the wider community to the
paper through the website and PDFs of each issue we are further able
to increase local audience and support.

<h3>Content Engagement</h3>

![Article containing results of a student survey we conducted.]({{"/images/phoneban.png" | relative_url}}){: style="float: right; margin-left: 1em; max-width:60%"}
<figcaption>Article containing results of a student survey we conducted on a recent change to the cellphone policy.</figcaption>
<br>

We have engaged our CVU community through a variety of mechanisms over
the past several years. Research for several of our articles has
included surveys of community members such as “Students React to New
Phone Ban” or “Life is a Highway: Implications of Car Dependency.”
This latter op-ed piece on car dependency garnered over 120 responses
for its survey.

I have sought to involve the larger high school community by inviting
groups and clubs to write their own articles. I am particularly proud
of the articles written by RedHawks Robotics Team, the compositions of
the Social Communications class, and a poem by the Peer Partnership
Program (see the section on Commitment to Diversity for the last
two). We are working with the Environmental Action club for an article
on Climate Change and our local impacts on the environment.
<br>

![Example of article solicited from the CVU community]({{"/images/robohawks.png" | relative_url}}){: style="float: right; margin-left: 1em;"}
<figcaption>Example of article solicited from the CVU community.</figcaption>

In honor of the Valentine’s Day issue in February 2025, I solicited a
“Community Call Outs,” where individuals could scan a QR code present
in the paper and on posters around the school in order to send kind
messages to individuals or groups across the school.

![Callouts submitted by the community]({{"/images/callouts.png" | relative_url}}){: style="max-width=60%; display:block"}
<figcaption>Callouts submitted by the community.</figcaption>
<br>

For nearly every issue, we have also had a resource available to
answer questions for any individual who may be grappling with social
issues around school in the form of an advice column known as “Dear
Amber.” It is run by an anonymous student who provides feedback and
encouragement to those seeking advice.

![Sample "Dear Amber" column with QR code for the submission of questions]({{"/images/amber.png" | relative_url}}){: style="float: right; margin-left: 1em;"}
<figcaption>Sample "Dear Amber" column with QR code for the submission of questions.</figcaption>
<br>

Finally, as a person who deeply appreciates puzzles, I have aimed to
make the printed issues interactive wherever possible. I have
constructed a crossword for every issue. In addition, several members
of the staff have created sudokus, trivia quizzes, and word searches
when space has allowed.

<h3>Partnerships</h3>

![The Citizen]({{"/images/citizen.png" | relative_url}}){: style="float: right; margin-left: 1em; max-width: 75%"}

Recently the Hinesburg _Citizen_, the newspaper covering the town in
which our high school resides, approached us to ask if we would like
to publish articles from _The Nest_ in the _Citizen_. A reporter from
_The Citizen_ came and presented at our club meeting and we have had
several discussions recently to work out the logistics. We are excited
about this new opportunity for our newspaper.

<h3>Local businesses</h3>

We have been graciously supported by our local businesses through
their efforts in providing us with ads and material for
fundraisers. In _The Nest_’s inaugural Winter Auction, fifteen local
businesses contributed to the fundraiser. I was able to obtain
products from thirteen of these fifteen businesses.

<h3>Marketing</h3>

Having a Business Manager is an essential part of _The Hawks’ Nest_’s
viability and sustainability. In partnership with our business
manager, we have been experimenting with the website: we initially
started with the CVU website and then transferred over to Netlify. To
keep costs down we may return to the CVU website. We have one
consistent advertisement, garnered by a managing editor early in our
second year, and have been looking to get more. This has been a
sustainable option and advertisements may be an important strategy in
future years. In addition, growing the newspaper’s digital presence
will certainly support marketing endeavors in the future.
